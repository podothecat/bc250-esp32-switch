Controller wake
Register the BC250 and your controllers so they can wake it. A PlayStation controller then wakes it with the PS button alone; an Xbox or other BLE controller wakes it when turned on. Registration never pairs a controller to this device, so it keeps its pairing with the BC250.
Do not pair a PlayStation controller to this device with Create + PS (PS5) or SHARE + PS (PS4): that replaces its pairing with the BC250. Use only the steps below.
1. Register the BC250
Checking…
On the BC250, open Steam → Settings → Bluetooth and select BC250 Power Setup. Steam will report that it could not connect — that is expected, and the address is captured anyway.
2. Register controllers
Shut the BC250 down from Steam first, then continue. On the next screen, turn on each controller one at a time — a PlayStation controller with its PS button, an Xbox controller with its small pairing button (top edge, next to the USB port) until it flashes quickly.
